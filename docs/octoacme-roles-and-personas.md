# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Engineering Manager / Delivery Lead

### Role Summary
Engineering Managers or Delivery Leads translate strategic priorities into executable team plans, support delivery health, and help the engineering organization operate effectively.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate team capacity, staffing, and sprint commitments
- Align engineering work with roadmap and milestone priorities
- Support technical planning, dependency resolution, and risk mitigation
- Mentor team members and reinforce delivery quality and accountability
- Partner with Product and Project Managers to keep execution realistic and predictable

### Goals
- Improve delivery predictability and team focus
- Resolve cross-team bottlenecks quickly and transparently
- Maintain high engineering quality while balancing scope and capacity

### Typical Communication
- Weekly engineering and cross-functional delivery syncs
- Dependency reviews and milestone planning discussions
- Escalation paths for delivery risks or resourcing challenges

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works closely with Product Managers to convert roadmap priorities into feasible delivery plans
- Supports Project Managers by validating schedule assumptions, capacity, and execution risks
- Coordinates with Developers to clarify technical trade-offs and unblock execution

---

## QA / Test Lead

### Role Summary
QA / Test Leads ensure that products meet quality standards, acceptance criteria, and release readiness requirements before features are delivered.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy, quality gates, and regression coverage requirements
- Validate acceptance criteria across functional and non-functional requirements
- Coordinate test planning for releases, enhancements, and critical workflows
- Identify quality risks early and drive remediation with engineering partners
- Partner with stakeholders to validate whether readiness criteria are met

### Goals
- Reduce production defects and quality surprises
- Improve confidence in release readiness and operational stability
- Ensure that user value is validated against agreed outcomes

### Typical Communication
- Sprint quality reviews and release readiness check-ins
- Test planning discussions with Developers and Product Managers
- Defect triage and risk status updates with stakeholders

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Developers to review defects, establish root cause, and validate fixes
- Aligns with Product Managers on acceptance criteria and release quality expectations
- Collaborates with Project Managers to ensure QA timelines and release dependencies are accurately reflected in plans

---

## Security / Compliance Partner

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance Partners help the team manage risk, protect sensitive information, and ensure projects meet required regulatory and governance expectations.

### Responsibilities
- Review architecture, workflows, and release plans for security and compliance considerations
- Identify policy, audit, and risk requirements relevant to the initiative
- Advise on secure design decisions and remediation priorities
- Support incident readiness, vulnerability triage, and response coordination
- Ensure key controls are in place before release or onboarding

### Goals
- Reduce risk exposure and improve trust in product delivery
- Ensure compliance standards are considered early and consistently
- Support safe, resilient release practices across the lifecycle

### Typical Communication
- Design and architecture review sessions
- Security risk and compliance status updates
- Incident response and escalation coordination

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Provides guidance to Developers and Engineering Managers on secure implementation choices
- Informs Product and Project Managers about risk exposure, remediation timing, or control requirements
- Partners with stakeholders to align business goals with compliance and security needs

---

## DevOps / Platform Engineer

### Role Summary
DevOps / Platform Engineers provide the infrastructure, delivery pipelines, and operational guardrails that enable reliable and repeatable deployment practices.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain environments, automation, and deployment pipelines
- Improve reliability, observability, and operational support for software delivery
- Support release readiness, rollback readiness, and production health monitoring
- Standardize platform patterns that reduce delivery friction for engineering teams
- Collaborate on infrastructure, scaling, and operational resilience decisions

### Goals
- Enable fast, low-risk, and repeatable delivery
- Improve platform stability and developer productivity
- Reduce operational firefighting and deployment uncertainty

### Typical Communication
- Release planning and deployment readiness reviews
- Infrastructure and platform status updates
- Operational incident coordination and postmortem follow-through

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Supports Developers by improving build, test, and deployment workflows
- Works with Project Managers and Product Managers to align rollout plans with operational constraints
- Coordinates with Security partners on platform controls and environment protections

---

## Customer / User Advocate or Stakeholder Liaison

### Role Summary
Customer or User Advocates represent end-user needs and stakeholder interests so project decisions stay grounded in customer value and organizational goals.

### Responsibilities
- Gather user and stakeholder feedback, needs, and pain points
- Translate customer context into product requirements and prioritization input
- Validate that solution decisions align with user goals and business priorities
- Support communication around roadmap changes, trade-offs, and adoption impact
- Help maintain a shared understanding of expected outcomes across teams

### Goals
- Keep delivery anchored to customer value and business impact
- Improve clarity and trust across stakeholder groups
- Ensure user concerns are visible during planning and release decisions

### Typical Communication
- Stakeholder updates, user research sessions, and feedback reviews
- Product prioritization and roadmap alignment discussions
- Release and adoption communications with business partners

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Provides input to Product Managers and Project Managers on priority, urgency, and stakeholder expectations
- Helps Developers and QA teams understand real-world usage and edge cases
- Supports leadership in clarifying the business value of project outcomes

---

## Data / Analytics Lead

### Role Summary
Data and Analytics Leads define how success will be measured, track project impact, and ensure that teams use evidence to guide iteration and decision-making.

### Responsibilities
- Define success metrics, instrumentation needs, and reporting requirements
- Monitor product usage, operational health, and business outcomes
- Support experimentation, feature validation, and adoption analysis
- Turn metrics into actionable insights for Product and delivery teams
- Help clarify whether project goals are being met over time

### Goals
- Improve decision quality through measurable evidence
- Connect delivery work to outcomes and customer value
- Support iteration based on observed impact rather than assumptions

### Typical Communication
- KPI tracking and weekly performance reviews
- Reporting to Product Managers, PMs, and leadership
- Experiment review and insight sharing across teams

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Product Managers to define meaningful success measures
- Supports Project Managers by surfacing execution and outcome risks early
- Helps Developers and QA teams understand what metrics matter for reliability and user value

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- These broader role definitions help teams map responsibilities more clearly across delivery, quality, security, operations, stakeholder engagement, and measurement.
